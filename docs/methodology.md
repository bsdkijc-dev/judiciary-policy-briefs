# Research Methodology & Verification Framework

Our workflow follows a transparent 12-stage pipeline from primary-source discovery to automated validation and reproducible publication.

- **Source Provenance**: Every observation traces to an official publication in `source_register.csv`.
- **Zero-Imputation Policy**: Missing values remain `NULL` (empty CSV cells) rather than being interpolated.
- **Six-Tier Verification Taxonomy**: `VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `DISCREPANCY IDENTIFIED`, `NOT AVAILABLE`, `NOT COMPARABLE`.
- **No Silent Reconciliation**: Arithmetic or narrative discrepancies (e.g., 2016 `16,233` vs. `16,223`; 2021→2022 opening balance `175` vs. `155`; 2025 national tier-sum `3,035,152` vs. `3,025,152`) are explicitly documented.
