# Evidence for Better Justice Policy

*Judicial open data, policy analysis, and institutional research for a more transparent and accessible justice system.*

Welcome to the **Indonesia Judiciary Open Data Initiative** (`bsdkijc-dev/judiciary-policy-briefs`), an independent research portfolio led by **Cecep Mustafa**. Our mission is to connect official institutional court reports to verified open datasets, reproducible Python analysis, and evidence-based policy briefs.

---

## Featured Policy Brief: `PB-JUD-2026-001`
**From Court Statistics to Better Justice Policy: Understanding Caseload, Case Clearance, and Pending Cases in Indonesia's Judiciary (2015–2025)**

![PB-JUD-2026-001 Cover](assets/images/cover.png)

- **+107.30% Caseload Growth**: Supreme Court (`MA-RI`) active caseload rose from `18,402` (2015) to `38,148` (2025).
- **-95.57% Backlog Reduction**: Year-end pending cases dropped from `3,950` (2015) to `175` (2025).
