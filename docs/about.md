# About the Initiative

The **Indonesia Judiciary Open Data Initiative** is an independent research and open-data engineering project led by **Cecep Mustafa** (`bsdkijc-dev` on GitHub; `judicialcollege` on Kaggle). It aims to strengthen evidence-based judicial policy analysis, open government data standards, and international research collaboration on access to justice and rule-of-law institutions.

## Institutional Independence & Disclaimer
This initiative is strictly independent. It is not officially endorsed by, nor does it represent the official views of, the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia (*Mahkamah Agung RI*), BSDK, IJC, or any other public institution.
