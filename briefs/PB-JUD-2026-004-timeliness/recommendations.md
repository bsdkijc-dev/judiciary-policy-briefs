# Policy Recommendations (`PB-JUD-2026-004`)

1. **Report End-to-End Disposition-to-Dispatch Timeliness**: Pair `<90`-day deciding metrics with `<96`-day minutation and dispatch distributions so litigants see total time to enforceable judgment.
2. **Standardize Minutation Threshold Definitions**: Reconcile `<=96` calendar days (`SK KMA 214/2014`) with `1–3` month reporting buckets to eliminate minor discrepancies (e.g., 2021 `16.73%` vs. `18.25%`).
3. **Extend Digital Timeliness Monitoring to Subordinate Tiers**: Replicate `SIAP-MA` + `RESPEK` delay-alert dashboards across appellate and first-instance `SIPP` systems.
