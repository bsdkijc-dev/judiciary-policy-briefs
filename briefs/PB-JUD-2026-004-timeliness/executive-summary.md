# Executive Summary (`PB-JUD-2026-004`)

**Court Efficiency, Timeliness, and Case Management: Benchmarking On-Time Case Processing and Minutation Turnaround**

- **Publication ID**: `PB-JUD-2026-004` | **Version**: `1.0.0` | **Date**: November 26, 2026
- **Author**: Cecep Mustafa | **Initiative**: Indonesia Judiciary Open Data Initiative
- **Disclaimer**: Independent research; not endorsed by the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia (*Mahkamah Agung RI*).

## Key Verified Findings
1. **Sustained High-Speed Deciding**: Under *SK KMA No. 214/KMA/SK/XII/2014* (90-day decision target), `MA-RI` on-time deciding rose from **80.75%** (2016) to **97.77%** (2021), **99.52%** (2025), and **99.78%** (2026 YTD).
2. **The Historical Minutation Bottleneck (2020–2021)**: Despite >96% deciding speed, on-time minutation (`<=96` days) stood at only **37.48%** in 2020 and dropped to **16.73%** in 2021 while the registry cleared **3,852** legacy >1-year minutation backlogs.
3. **Registry Turnaround (2022–2026)**: Digital workflow reforms, e-Kasasi/e-PK, and continuous monitoring lifted 1–3 month minutation timeliness from **64.51%** (2022) to **90.32%** (2023), **93.93%** (2024), and **97.01%** (2026 YTD)—closing the gap between judicial decision and judgment dispatch.
