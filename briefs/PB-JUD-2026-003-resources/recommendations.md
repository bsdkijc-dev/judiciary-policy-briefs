# Policy Recommendations (`PB-JUD-2026-003`)

1. **Adopt Workload-Weighted Judicial Staffing Models**: Move beyond raw headcount ratios by weighting 3-judge panel allocations against case complexity across First Instance (1,247 files/judge) and Supreme Court (2,384 files/justice) tiers.
2. **Close the Supreme Court Bench Gap**: Synchronize judicial selection with retirements to keep `MA-RI` closer to its 60-Justice statutory ceiling rather than oscillating between 45 and 48 Justices.
3. **Pair Budget Realization with Unit-Cost Transparency**: Publish per-case operational cost benchmarks alongside aggregate DIPA realization rates (98.12% in 2025) to guide capital and digital-justice investments.
