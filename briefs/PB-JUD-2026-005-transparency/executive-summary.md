# Executive Summary (`PB-JUD-2026-005`)

**Judicial Transparency and Public Accountability: Evaluating Decision Publication, Chamber Harmonization, and Anti-Bribery Governance**

- **Publication ID**: `PB-JUD-2026-005` | **Version**: `1.0.0` | **Date**: November 26, 2026
- **Author**: Cecep Mustafa | **Initiative**: Indonesia Judiciary Open Data Initiative
- **Disclaimer**: Independent research; not endorsed by the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia (*Mahkamah Agung RI*).

## Key Verified Findings
1. **355% Expansion in Open Decisions**: Published rulings on *Direktori Putusan* grew from **2,061,320** (2016) to **6,626,144** (2021) and **9,382,440** (2024), adding nearly **1 million** decisions per year (`980,654` in 2021; `951,970` in 2024).
2. **Jurisprudential Consistency**: Across 14 *Pleno Kamar* cycles (2012–2025), MA-RI issued **576** legal formulations, cited in **59,130** published decisions (`45,720` citing *Rapat Pleno Kamar* SEMA across 907 satkers + `13,410` citing *Rumusan Kamar* across 731 satkers).
3. **Digital Accountability & Anti-Bribery Controls**: By Nov 2026, **99.12%** of cassation/PK appeals were filed electronically, reinforced by `SK KMA 15/2025` (conflict of interest), Kepaniteraan's `SMAP` anti-bribery system (March 2025), and `RESPEK` complaint integration into `SIAP-MA`.
