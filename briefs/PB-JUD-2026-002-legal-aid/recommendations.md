# Policy Recommendations (`PB-JUD-2026-002`)

1. **Ring-Fence Prodeo & Posbakum Appropriations**: Exempt *PERMA 1/2014* court fee-waiver and Posbakum allocations from across-the-board fiscal efficiency freezes so low-income litigants do not lose access to courts mid-year.
2. **Harmonize Reporting Across MA-RI (4 Jurisdictions) and BPHN**: Publish unified annual open-data tables linking BPHN `SIDBANKUM` litigation/non-litigation counts with Badilum, Badilag, and Badilmiltun Posbakum, Prodeo, and Sidang Keliling metrics.
3. **Track Geographic & Vulnerable-Group Equity**: Disaggregate Posbakum and OBH coverage by province, rural/remote districts, and vulnerable-group categories (women, children, persons with disabilities) to address the informal-channel gap.
4. **Measure Unmet Legal Need**: Pair institutional service counts with periodic demand-side surveys building on the 2021 Access to Justice Index baseline (51.8 legal assistance score).
