# Executive Summary (`PB-JUD-2026-002`)

**Measuring Access to Justice and Legal Aid: Evaluating Posbakum, Prodeo, and Legal Aid Delivery Across Indonesia**

- **Publication ID**: `PB-JUD-2026-002` | **Version**: `1.0.0` | **Date**: November 26, 2026
- **Author**: Cecep Mustafa | **Initiative**: Indonesia Judiciary Open Data Initiative
- **Disclaimer**: Independent research; not endorsed by the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia (*Mahkamah Agung RI*) or BPHN.

## Key Verified Findings
1. **Dual-Track Legal Aid Architecture**: Indonesia splits state-funded legal aid between **BPHN** (*UU No. 16 Tahun 2011*, funding accredited OBHs for litigation and non-litigation representation) and **Mahkamah Agung RI** (*PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2014*, funding court fee waivers/*Prodeo*, mobile courts/*Sidang di Luar Gedung*, and court legal aid posts/*Posbakum*).
2. **Accredited OBH Expansion**: BPHN-accredited Legal Aid Organizations rose **+25.53%**, from **619 OBHs** (2022–2024) to **777 OBHs** (2025–2027).
3. **Unmet Litigation Demand**: In 2024, BPHN fulfilled **14,104** of **16,570** SIDBANKUM litigation applications (**85.12%** fulfillment rate), leaving **2,466** applications unfulfilled.
4. **Court-Based Reach & Fiscal Vulnerability**: General Courts (*Badilum*) served **44,491** Posbakum beneficiaries in 2024, and MA-RI reached **22,748** Prodeo cases in 2025. However, because *PERMA 1/2014* conditions fee waivers on DIPA budget availability, national fiscal efficiency directives (*Inpres No. 1 Tahun 2025* and *Surat Sekma No. 266/SEK/RA1.8/II/2025*) led local courts such as PA Pasarwajo to temporarily suspend Prodeo budgets in early 2025.
