# Policy Recommendations (`PB-JUD-2026-006`)

1. **Close the Remaining 0.88% Regional Digital Divide**: Target bandwidth, hardware, and assisted e-Court kiosks in remote jurisdictions where manual appeal filings persist.
2. **Mandate Open Interoperable APIs & Data Standards**: Standardize schemas across `e-Court`, `e-Berpadu`, `SIAP-MA`, and `Direktori Putusan` to support longitudinal policy analytics.
3. **Embed Human-in-the-Loop AI Governance**: Require deterministic statutory verification and judicial human-review gates (e.g., `JPAIS` Decision-Before-Assistance) before deploying AI research tools.
