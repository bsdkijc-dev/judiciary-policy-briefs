# Dataset: `PB-JUD-2026-002` — Measuring Access to Justice and Legal Aid in Indonesia

- **Version**: `1.0.0` | **Author**: Cecep Mustafa (`bsdkijc-dev` / Kaggle: `judicialcollege`)
- **Scope**: Court-based legal aid under *PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2014* (Posbakum, Prodeo) and national legal aid under *UU No. 16 Tahun 2011* (BPHN accredited OBHs and SIDBANKUM litigation fulfillment).
