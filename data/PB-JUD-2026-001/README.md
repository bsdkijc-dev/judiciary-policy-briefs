# Dataset: `PB-JUD-2026-001` — Indonesia Judiciary Caseload and Clearance Statistics (2015–2025)

- **Version**: `1.0.0`
- **Author**: Cecep Mustafa (`bsdkijc-dev` / Kaggle: `judicialcollege`)
- **Files**:
  - `judicial_caseload.csv`: 19 verified/audited records covering MA-RI (2015–2025) and national/tier snapshots (2017, 2020, 2024, 2025).
  - `source_register.csv`: 18 official institutional sources with URLs and verification status.
  - `data_dictionary.csv`: Complete variable definitions, units, and standards.
  - `validation_status.csv`: Automated output of `scripts/validate_data.py`.
  - `summary_statistics.json`: Reproducible metrics generated by `scripts/generate_statistics.py`.

## Independent Research Disclaimer
This dataset is an independent open-data research compilation derived from public Mahkamah Agung RI publications. It is not an official publication of, nor endorsed by, the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia.
