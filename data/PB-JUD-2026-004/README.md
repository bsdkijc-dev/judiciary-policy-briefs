# Dataset: `PB-JUD-2026-004` — Court Efficiency, Timeliness, and Case Management (2016–2026 YTD)

- **Version**: `1.0.0` | **Author**: Cecep Mustafa (`bsdkijc-dev` / Kaggle: `judicialcollege`)
- **Scope**: Longitudinal comparison of Supreme Court (`MA-RI`) on-time deciding (`<3 months`) vs. on-time minutation (`<=96 days` / `1-3 months`) under *SK KMA No. 214/KMA/SK/XII/2014*.
- **Files**: `timeliness_metrics.csv` (15 audited records), `source_register.csv` (10 official Kepaniteraan MA-RI sources), `data_dictionary.csv`.
