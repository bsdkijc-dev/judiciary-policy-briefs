# Methodological Limitations

1. **Aggregate Scope**: `PB-JUD-2026-001` v1.0.0 analyzes Supreme Court (MA-RI) longitudinal data (2015–2025) and selected national/tier snapshots (2017, 2020, 2024, 2025). It does not yet disaggregate all 800+ individual first-instance and appellate courts across every year.
2. **Minutation vs. Disposition Lag**: High *Rasio Produktivitas Memutus* (>99% at MA-RI since 2020) measures judicial decision-making (*memutus*), not always administrative completion and dispatch (*minutasi dan kirim*). When minutation lags, litigants still experience delayed access to final written judgments.
3. **Case Complexity Unweighted**: Raw case counts treat simple procedural dismissals and complex multi-party commercial or corruption appeals equally.
4. **Descriptive Scope**: Observed correlations between caseload growth, digital reforms (e-Court, e-Kasasi), and pending-case reductions do not establish formal causal proof without case-level microdata.
